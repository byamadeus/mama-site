# Background research: Tajči / Tatiana "Tajci" Cameron

Compiled from public web search (July 2026) to give future sessions working
on this site real biographical grounding instead of re-deriving it each
time. **This is secondhand, public-web research, not vetted by the family
— confirm anything before it goes on the live site**, especially the
sensitive items flagged below.

## Quick facts

- Full name: Tatjana Cameron (née Matejaš), performs as **Tajči** / **Tajci**
  (no diacritic in most US/English-language use, including her own site
  copy) — born July 1, 1970, in Croatia (then part of Yugoslavia).
- Sons: Dante, Evan, and Blais Cameron. (This session's user account email
  is `evanamadeuscameron@gmail.com` — almost certainly her son Evan, i.e.
  this is a family-run project, not an agency/label engagement.)
- Based in Franklin, Tennessee since 2014.

## Music career

- Teenage pop star in Yugoslavia/Croatia in the late 1980s: sold over a
  million records, multiple platinum certifications, national pop icon by
  age 19.
- Represented **Yugoslavia at Eurovision Song Contest 1990** (held in
  Zagreb, the host country that year) with the song **"Hajde da
  ludujemo"** ("Let's Go Crazy") — finished 7th.
- Yugoslav career cut short by the breakup of Yugoslavia and the wars;
  left the country in 1991–92.
- Moved to the US, studied at the American Musical and Dramatic Academy
  in New York City.
- Continues to perform — pop and jazz, described elsewhere as ranging
  from Carnegie Hall to intimate jazz clubs — and has collaborated with
  Grammy-winning musicians.

## Life in the US / family

- Married **Matthew Cameron** in 1999 (they met in church); he was also
  her partner and booking agent, and reportedly encouraged her to start
  her Patreon.
- **One source refers to Matthew Cameron as "her late husband."** This is
  a significant, sensitive fact this project has not otherwise mentioned —
  **do not add anything about this to site copy without the family's
  explicit direction.** Treat with care in any conversation about the
  site too.
- Relocated to Franklin, TN in 2014, the same year she began producing
  and hosting the TV show *Waking Up in America* (three seasons).

## Books

- Published five multimedia books total.
- Most recent: **"(un)Broken"** — combines poetry and song, drawing on
  diary entries, about how music and the creative process can help heal
  emotional pain.
- **"Turning Points: Stories from the TV show 'Waking Up in America'"** —
  sold as a digital guide/book.

## Coaching

- Certified life coach via the **Radiant Coaching Institute** and the
  **Chopra Center**.
- Also holds **PCC (Professional Certified Coach)** and **NBC-HWC**
  (National Board-Certified Health & Wellness Coach) credentials per her
  own site copy.
- Founder of **Waking UP Revolution**, described as a "multi-platform
  brand offering tools to transform lives."
- One interview (Serbian Times) has her discussing having experienced
  sexual violence before winning Eurovision, and now working with women
  who've experienced similar trauma. **Sensitive — confirm with the
  family before referencing this anywhere on the site**, even though it's
  something she has spoken about publicly herself.

## Speaking / mentoring / ministry

- Motivational/keynote speaker.
- **Board member, Nashville Peacemakers.**
- **"Better Decisions" volunteer mentor at Tennessee State Prison for
  Women** — this is almost certainly the "mentoring women in prison" work
  referenced in the original project brief.
- Volunteer presenter for **EndSlaveryTN**.
